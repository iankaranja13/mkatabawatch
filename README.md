# MkatabaWatch 🇹🇿 — Public Contracts, Public Evidence

> **Connecting official Tanzanian public procurement records with citizen and community monitor evidence, powered by AI reconciliation.**

MkatabaWatch bridges the gap between official government procurement data and on-the-ground reality in Tanzania. It ingests verified Open Contracting Data Standard (OCDS) releases from Tanzania's Public Procurement Regulatory Authority (PPRA) National e-Procurement System (NeST) portal, invites citizens and civil society monitors to submit geotagged photographic and observational evidence, and applies AI reconciliation to transparently flag discrepancies for human review.

## 🔍 Core Loop
1. **Official Record:** Ingested from real, live OCDS data published by Tanzania PPRA / NeST.
2. **Community Evidence:** Geotagged, timestamped photo and status observations submitted by citizens and community monitors (clearly designated between real and seeded demo records).
3. **AI Reconciliation:** AI side-by-side comparison identifying points of consistency or discrepancies without accusing anyone of corruption or asserting violations as fact.
4. **Human Verification Queue:** Flags inconsistencies for independent field verification by human auditors and civil society organizations.
